Woolen — Furniture E-Commerce

A sleek bento-style catalog & e-commerce layout for modern home furniture and interior pieces.

Overview

Woolen is a modern furniture and interior e-commerce web interface designed to showcase deals, popular chairs, custom studio options, and curated collections[cite: 17]. Built with a responsive bento-grid layout, the platform organizes products into distinct, visually engaging cards for an effortless browsing experience[cite: 17].

Role

UI/UX Designer & Frontend Developer — Designed and developed the website's dark/light contrast bento layout, responsive catalog cards, interactive search bar, and clean product discovery components[cite: 17].

Features

  • Bento Grid Layout: Organizes furniture deals, custom options, and popular chairs into a structured card interface[cite: 17].
  • Catalog Search & Filters: Quick navigation tags for Sofas, Tables, Dressers, Chairs, Beds, and Lamps[cite: 17].
  • Deal Highlights & Discounts: Displays promotional banners with clear pricing tags and custom dimension options[cite: 17].
  • Visual Product Cards: High-resolution imagery combined with minimal typography for quick browsing[cite: 17].
  • Responsive Layout: Adapts layout dynamically from desktop down to mobile devices[cite: 17].
